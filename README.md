# Bank Loan Data Analysis Dashboard (Excel)

📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes bank loan data to evaluate financial performance, customer behavior, and risk factors using an interactive Excel dashboard.

 🔍 Key Insights

* Total Loan Applications: 38.6K
* Total Funded Amount: $435.76M
* Total Amount Received: $473.07M
* Good Loans: 86.18%
* Bad Loans: 13.82%

 📈 Business Insights

* Loan demand increases steadily throughout the year
* Credit card loans dominate, indicating debt consolidation trends
* Customers with long employment history are key borrowers
* Renters and mortgage holders form the majority of customers
* Strong loan recovery indicates profitable operations

💡 Business Recommendations

* Enhance credit risk assessment to reduce bad loans
* Target stable and long-term employed customers
* Focus on high-performing regions
* Optimize loan offerings and interest rates
